### 3.3 Crucial Technical Distinction: Determinism vs. Correctness
A fundamental distinction must be maintained between **repeatability (determinism)** and **predictive correctness**:
- **What Repeatability Proves:** Model inference on CUDA is mathematically deterministic under standard evaluation settings (dropout inactive, batch normalization using running statistics, fixed weights, deterministic CuDNN operations).
- **What Repeatability Does NOT Prove:** Determinism does not imply accuracy. A model can be 100% repeatable while predicting the wrong class.
- **Empirical Demonstration (`CV00002.jpg`):**
  - Ground truth: `Curl Virus`.
  - Model prediction: `Leaf Redding` (40.74%), with `Curl Virus` trailing at 39.20%.
  - The model was completely incorrect on this sample, yet its incorrect prediction and exact probability vector were replicated with 0.00000000 drift across all 5 runs.
  - This demonstrates that low confidence correctly signals model uncertainty, but determinism alone cannot be conflated with diagnostic correctness.

## 4. Segmentation Metric Provenance & Reconciliation

Four different U-Net Macro Dice values appear across project records: **68.45%**, **70.46%**, **71.11%**, and **79.15%**. Each corresponds to a specific evaluation configuration, resolution, transform pipeline, and metric calculation method.

| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |

| **70.46%** | 0.5741 | Validation (15 pairs) | 512 $\times$ 512 | PIL Bilinear resize, `/255.0` (No ImageNet Norm) | Evaluated at 512 $\times$ 512 tensor grid | `compute_segmentation_metrics` on concatenated batches | `segmentation_eval_results.json` (`evaluate_segmentation_independent.py`) |
| **71.11%** | 0.5677 | Validation (15 pairs) | 256 $\times$ 256 | Torchvision Resize(256), ImageNet Norm | Nearest-Neighbor upsampled to native image size | Global pixel-level confusion matrix aggregation | `unet_resolution_comparison.json` (`compare_unet_resolutions.py`) |
| **79.15%** | 0.6698 | Validation (15 pairs) | 512 $\times$ 512 | Albumentations Resize(512), ImageNet Norm | Nearest-Neighbor upsampled to native image size | Global pixel-level confusion matrix aggregation | `unet_resolution_comparison.json` (`compare_unet_resolutions.py`) |

### 4.2 Detailed Technical Reconciliation of Discrepancies

1. **Why 70.46% vs. 79.15% (Transform Sensitivity):**
   - In `evaluate_segmentation_independent.py` (which produced 70.46%), images were loaded via PIL, resized to 512x512, and converted to tensors using `img_arr / 255.0` **without applying ImageNet standardization** (`mean=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406], std=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]`).
   - In `compare_unet_resolutions.py` (which produced 79.15%), images were processed with Albumentations `A.Normalize`, matching the exact training distribution.
   - Missing ImageNet normalization caused a 8.69 percentage point drop in Macro Dice, primarily harming the minority diseased tissue class (Dice dropped from 62.55% to 43.82%).

2. **Why 71.11% vs. 79.15% (Resolution Downsampling Loss):**
   - Both evaluations applied proper ImageNet normalization and nearest-neighbor upsampling to native image resolution.
   - At 256x256 (the current API service transform), high-frequency lesion borders and angular leaf margins are blurred or lost in downsampling, reducing Diseased Tissue Dice from 62.55% to 52.07% and Macro Dice from 79.15% to 71.11% (-8.04 percentage points).

3. **Why 68.45% in Checkpoint Metadata:**
   - 68.45% was recorded at Epoch 31 during model training. It reflects the mean validation Dice evaluated across fixed 512x512 patches on the fly without post-hoc nearest-neighbor full-resolution pixel mapping.

## 5. Unresolved Architectural & Benchmark Limitations

1. **Absence of External Benchmark Dataset:**
   - The entire labelled corpus of cotton leaf images in the workspace (2,132 images in SAR-CLD) is exhausted across `train.csv`, `validation.csv`, and `test.csv`.
   - No separate, out-of-distribution external dataset exists on disk.
   - **Status:** True out-of-distribution generalization remains **unverified** until external field photography is acquired.

2. **U-Net Service Resolution Mismatch:**
   - The U-Net model was trained at 512x512 with Albumentations normalization.
   - The live backend inference service (`src/inference/segmentation_service.py`) processes images at 256x256 using Torchvision transforms.
   - This resolution discrepancy causes an 8.04% Macro Dice degradation in production. Resolving this discrepancy is cataloged for Phase 12.
