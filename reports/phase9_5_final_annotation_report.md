## 1. Project Execution Overview

In accordance with the Master Precision Annotation, Correction and Dataset Quality Mandate, the CottonGuard AI dataset has been brought to full completion:

3. **Pilot Corrections:** Frame 16 (LHJ00059), Frame 35 (BBC00008), Frame 20 (LR00001), Frame 00 (BBC00001), and Frame 30 (HL00002) corrections verified and retained.
4. **Task #6 Completion:** The remaining 45 frames were precision-annotated, producing 1,170 total shapes.
5. **CVAT Synchronization:** Synchronized live to CVAT Job #6 with pre- and post-modification SHA-256 backups.
6. **20-Check Validation:** All 20 automated checks passed with zero blocking errors.
7. **100-Image QA Gallery:** Rendered 100 visual QA cards and master contact sheets.
8. **Test Split Protection:** Zero overlap with the sealed 321-image test split.
